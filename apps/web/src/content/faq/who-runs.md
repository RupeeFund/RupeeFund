**Volunteers** from the FOSS United community, with experience in many fields, run it. You can meet them on the <a href="/people" class="inline-link">People</a> page. When contributions open, the money will go into a FOSS United Foundation account. The Foundation also runs its own grants programme, which has given more than **₹3.3 crore to 135 grantees**: projects, events and fellowships.
