question: What do you do with my signup details?
order: 13
---

We will send you **one email when the fund opens**, and monthly updates if you tick that box. We also use your answers to plan the fund.

Your answers about money are **not a commitment**. We do not need any payment details today: you are only showing your support for the mission of this fund.

Read our <a href="/privacy" class="inline-link">privacy policy</a> for more.
