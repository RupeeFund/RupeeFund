question: What kind of projects will be funded?
order: 2
home: true
---

Indian individuals and projects that work in the open, as **free and open source**: anyone can use, study, change and share their work, for any purpose.

It can be software, hardware, or digital and physical designs: from high-tech chips to the cloud, from devices to tech art. Community efforts built on the same principles count too.

Each project must use a well-known free and open source licence. The fund can pay a person, a group or an organisation. They must also be able to **receive money over UPI**.

_You_ and other contributors nominate and choose the projects.
