Those are large grant programmes, and each one is separate. **FOSS United grants** have given more than ₹3.3&nbsp;crore to 135 grantees in India. **FLOSS/fund**, from Zerodha, gives up to US$1&nbsp;million a year to free and open source projects around the world. The&nbsp;Rupee&nbsp;Fund works differently: volunteers run it, and **many of us each give a little**.
