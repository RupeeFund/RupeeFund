<Steps title="How it works" seasonsTitle="Funding seasons">

<Step n={1} title="Subscribe">

Set up a **monthly** contribution with UPI AutoPay. Small, regular contributions from many of us keep the fund steady.

</Step>

<Step n={2} title="Pool funds">

Your contributions **pool** through the season. The fund runs four cycles a year, following the Indian weather seasons.

</Step>

<Step n={3} title="Vote and nominate">

Contribute regularly and you earn a **vote** each season. You decide how to split it across three kinds of projects. You may also **nominate** one project or person each season.

</Step>

<Step n={4} title="Disburse">

Each season, the pool **goes** to the projects that you chose, over UPI. We publish every administrative cost, so you can follow your money all the way to a project.

</Step>

</Steps>
